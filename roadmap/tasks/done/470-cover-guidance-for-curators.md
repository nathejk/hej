## Description

The maintainer's condition on the album decision: *"we can add some descriptions in the admin pages for how to choose
cover photo and what to be aware of."*

The album editor card gains a short explanation beside **Gør til forsidebillede**: the cover is what the frontpage
shows, it is what a shared link shows on Facebook, and **a photograph shared onward is copied to Facebook and cannot
be recalled from there** — so pick one the patrol would be happy to see passed around, and prefer a wide shot to a
close-up of one child's face.

Danish. The same point goes in `docs/billedarkiv-for-fotografer.md` §4, where the cover is already explained.

This is the one place somebody can act on the cost PRD 026 accepted, which is why it is a requirement and not a
nicety.
